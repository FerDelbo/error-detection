Concrete examples from your data:

Detection methods:
- Regex validation:

- Flag all values not matching the pattern as Pattern Violations.
- Optional: normalize whitespace and case (e.g., lower-case) before applying the regex.

Remediation approaches:

- For values that don’t match (e.g., "6xpatients"), attempt a safe auto-correction:
