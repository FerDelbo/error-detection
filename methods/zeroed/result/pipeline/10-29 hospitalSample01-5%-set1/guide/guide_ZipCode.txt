1) Meaning of the ZipCode attribute

- What ZipCode represents: In the U.S., ZipCode is the postal code used by the United States Postal Service to designate delivery routes and geographic areas. It is typically a 5-digit numeric code, sometimes extended to ZIP+4 (five digits, a dash, and four more digits). In most healthcare analytics, ZipCode is used to geolocate facilities, link to county/state demographics, validate address quality, and enable location-based analytics (e.g., regional performance, payer networks, or health outcomes by region).

- How it’s stored in this dataset: ZipCode is recorded as a string (text) field, e.g., '35976', '36467', '35233'. Treat it as a fixed-length identifier rather than a numeric measure (to preserve leading zeros if ever present and to ease string comparisons).

- Important cross-checks to consider (not all are visible in the sample but are standard practice):
  - ZipCode format: 5 digits (or 5+4 when applicable).
  - State alignment: ZipCode ranges belong to particular states (e.g., Alabama ZIPs generally start with 3).
  - Geographic consistency: City and County should map plausibly to the ZipCode and State.
  - Data quality: ZipCode should not be empty or malformed; it should be present where location matters.
